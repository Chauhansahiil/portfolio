IDfy is hiring a Entry Level Data Analyst in Nagpur. This is a full-time, permanent role for candidates who want to work on real data analytics problems.
About the role
This Entry Level Data Analyst role is hands-on and delivery-focused. Expect an entry-level analytics seat — SQL, Excel and Power BI applied to real reporting from day one, working primarily in SQL, Excel and Power BI.
Key responsibilities
• Prepare and clean datasets for the analytics team under a senior analyst
• Build simple dashboards and recurring reports, reviewed before release
• Run first-pass analyses and summarise what the data shows
• Cross-check published numbers against source systems
• Document your queries and steps so the work is repeatable
• Automate recurring reporting so the team spends its time on analysis rather than refreshes
• Present findings to non-technical stakeholders in clear, plain language
Required skills and tools

• Advanced Excel
• Power BI
• Tableau
• Python (Pandas)
• Data Cleaning
• Data Visualization
• Descriptive Statistics
• Dashboarding
• Business Acumen
• Stakeholder Communication
• Documentation
