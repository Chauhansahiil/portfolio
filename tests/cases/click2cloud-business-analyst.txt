* Requirement Analysis: Gather, analyze, and validate business and technical requirements from various stakeholders and ensure alignment with business objectives.

Elicitation: Conduct interviews, workshops, surveys, and document analysis to effectively elicit and clarify requirements from stakeholders.
Modeling: Develop models (e.g., process flows, data models, use cases, user stories) to represent requirements and business processes accurately.
Verification & Validation: Ensure that requirements are complete, consistent, and meet the business needs through review, traceability, and user acceptance testing
Methodology Development: Define, implement, and continuously improve business analysis frameworks, best practices, and standards to support project success.
Stakeholder Collaboration: Act as a liaison between business users, development teams, and project managers to ensure smooth communication and effective solution delivery
Documentation: Prepare clear, concise, and comprehensive business requirements documents (BRDs), functional specifications, and other related documentation.
